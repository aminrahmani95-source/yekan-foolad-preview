Company profile

Yekan Foolad Co.

Since 1994, Yekan Foolad has delivered industrial projects across Iran. Its record covers mechanical and electrical installation, structural steel, piping, electrical and mechanical equipment and instrumentation for refineries and petrochemical plants, the fabrication and erection of power-plant equipment, and the construction of stacks and storage tanks.

Heavy structural steel fabricated to close tolerances, ducting, civil works and industrial buildings also form part of the delivery record. Yekan Foolad has executed its projects entirely as principal.
